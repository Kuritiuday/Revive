Team : Compiling Chaos


Abstract: 
ReVyana is a curated e-commerce platform designed to promote sustainable living by offering reusable and eco-friendly products. It empowers consumers to make conscious choices through verified product listings, educational content, and a seamless shopping experience. The platform aims to reduce environmental impact while making sustainability accessible and engaging.


Vision: 
"To create a world where sustainable living is effortless, accessible, and embraced by all."


Mission:
“Our mission is to build a trusted platform that curates Reusable and Eco-friendly products, educates consumers on sustainable living, and fosters a community committed to protecting the planet—one conscious choice at a time.”
Objective:
•	To simplify eco-conscious shopping by curating verified bio-degradable products
•	To educate users on sustainability and responsible consumption
•	To build a community-driven platform that encourages long-term environmental impact
•	To demonstrate a scalable, tech-enabled solution 


Key Features:
•	Make your own product with customization
•	Rewarding system 
•	Sustainability scores and traceable sourcing information
•	Secure checkout and responsive design


Design and User Experience:
•	Clean, minimalist UI with earthy tones to reflect eco values
•	Intuitive navigation with category-based browsing
•	Responsive design for accessibility
•	User reviews focused on environmental impact
•	Seamless onboarding and checkout flow


Tech Stack:
•	UI/UX Design : Figma
•	Frontend: React.js
•	Backend: Node.js + Express.js
•	Payments: WhatsApp Business and UPI
•	Hosting: Vercel/Render


Innovation and Impact:
•	Exclusive focus on eco-friendly and reusable products
•	Educational content embedded in the shopping experience
•	Potential to gamify eco-actions and reward users
•	Supports global sustainability goals and conscious consumerism


Future Scope:
•	Launch mobile app with gamified eco-rewards
•	Experiencing products in 360-degree view
•	Introduce community features and eco-challenges
•	Partner with NGOs and green brands for impact campaigns
•	Explore international expansion with localized sourcing


Conclusion:
ReVive is more than a marketplace—it’s a movement toward mindful living. By combining technology, education, and community, it offers a scalable solution to a pressing global issue. The hackathon prototype demonstrates both technical feasibility and market relevance, with a clear roadmap for future growth.
